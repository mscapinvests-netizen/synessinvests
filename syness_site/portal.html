Multilingual intake · English / Français. Everything you share is treated as confidential and used only to design the student's pathway strategy.

When should you start? Ideally, university planning begins during Première / Year 12 (or the equivalent), allowing time to strengthen grades, choose subjects strategically, prepare language tests, build extracurricular evidence, research destinations and prepare applications. However, Syness can assess students at any stage — early planning, late applications, transfers, mature-student entry, postgraduate study or urgent pathway review. Earlier preparation creates more options.

The Digital Client Journey — Portal Areas

Your Syness environment, once onboarded.

Illustrative preview of the family dashboard modules. Live activation follows onboarding and connection to Syness's secure case-management system.

My Profile

Student snapshot: identity, curriculum, academic profile, languages, career vision, destinations and family decision-making — exactly as captured in the intake above, kept current with your adviser.

Demo view — populated after your profile is received.

My Pathways

After the diagnostic consultation, your adviser publishes your three routes — Aspirational · Strategic · Secure — with recommended institutions, entry requirements, gaps to close and the rationale for each pathway.

Demo view — published after your pathway report.

My Applications

Submission order, deadlines, internal Syness target dates and live status for UCAS and direct applications — coordinated so nothing is missed.

Demo view — activated when applications begin.

My Documents

Secure upload for transcripts, school reports, references, translations and identity documents, with a checklist of what is missing.

Demo view — secure vault connected at onboarding.

My Tests

English and admissions-test planning: diagnostic level, target score, preparation timetable, approved test route and retake strategy.

Demo view — planned during the diagnostic.

My Offers

Offers received, compared strategically against your pathways, budget and long-term goals — with decision support before deadlines.

Demo view — appears with your first offers.

My Visa Checklist

Document coordination for the visa process — factual checklists and deadlines, prepared with your adviser. Immigration decisions remain with the relevant authorities.

Demo view — opened at offer stage.

My Accommodation

Accommodation strategy and transition planning for your destination — options, deadlines and arrival logistics.

Demo view — planned pre-departure.

My Appointments

Diagnostic consultation, strategy sessions, pre-departure briefing and check-ins — booked and tracked here.

Demo view — scheduling opens after submission.

Family Dashboard

One view for parents and guardians: progress, payment milestones, upcoming decisions, communication with the Syness team and urgent contacts.
